For you, Aanya

Happy birthday, you wonderful human.

I wanted to make you something that felt a little like you — warm, a bit silly, and full of tiny surprises. So here it is: a small corner of the internet that exists only because you do.

Thank you for every late-night talk, every ridiculous inside joke, every time you showed up without being asked. You make ordinary days feel like something worth remembering.

This year, I hope you get the slow mornings, the loud laughs, the good news you’ve been waiting for, and at least one perfect slice of cake.

Whatever you wished for back there — I hope it finds you soon. And if it takes a little while, I’ll be right here, cheering the loudest.

Happy birthday. Today is all yours.

Someone who loves you dearly ♡
